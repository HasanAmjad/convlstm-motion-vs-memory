# Q2.11 — Predictions for models B, C, D (written before training them)

Model B (symmetry augmentation) will close most of the mirror and rotation gap (lower C_M, C_R and E_M, E_R close to E(5) at 1×) but leave the 2× and 3× failure unchanged. Model C (speed augmentation) will fix 2× and improve 3× without fully fixing it, because 10 px/frame is still beyond its 8 px reach. Model D (larger reach, speed augmentation) will fix 3× as well, and neither C nor D will fix mirror or rotation.
