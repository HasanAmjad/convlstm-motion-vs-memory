# Q2.5 — Predictions for model A (written before *Testing the Baseline*)

Reach of model A (Q2.3): about 8 px per time step. Typical training speed (Figure 1): 3.3 px/frame (10–90%: 2.1–4.1).

- **Mirror:** passes, mostly. Mirrored motion is already common in training (Figure 1a covers all directions), so the motion should carry over. Mirrored digits look unfamiliar, so expect some blurring of digit shape, and C_M small but not zero.
- **Rotation:** passes the motion part but worse than mirror. Rotated motion is also familiar, but a sideways digit is less like any training digit than a mirrored one, so C_R > C_M.
- **Speed:** 2× (≈ 6.6 px/frame) is inside the 8 px reach but above every training speed, so predictions degrade but still follow the digits. 3× (≈ 10 px/frame) is beyond the reach, so the model breaks down there: digits stall or fade near their last input position.
